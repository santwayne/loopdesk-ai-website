AI Growth Manager

The part that runs your follow-ups for you

Most software waits for you to log in. Loopdesk AI reads your calls, texts, quotes, jobs and reviews every night, and asks you each morning before it acts.

  1. WatchEvery call, text, quote, job and review lands on one timeline per customer.
  2. DecideIt ranks the moves most likely to bring in work: a missed lead, a quiet quote, a service that's due, a regular who stopped coming.
  3. AskAt 8 am you get a numbered plan by text. Reply with numbers, ALL or SKIP, or edit any message on the web first.
  4. ActThe AI calls back, texts reminders, replies to Google reviews and drafts posts, only inside your business hours.
  5. LearnWhen someone books or pays, the action that won them gets the credit, and next week's plan leans on what worked.
