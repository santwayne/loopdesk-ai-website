Questions

Before you ask

Everything owners usually ask before starting a trial. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to the front desk, all the time or only when you're busy or closed. Texts go out from a local Canadian number, or from your own number where it can be enabled.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It takes the caller's name, number and what they need, tells them someone will call back, and texts you the details straight away.

Does the AI contact my customers without asking?

Not unless you let it. Every action waits for your OK by default: reply to the morning text or tap Approve. Once you trust it, you can let it send routine messages like review requests on its own. It only contacts customers during your business hours, never more than twice a week, and AI calls go only to people who called you or are already your customers.

Do I need separate logins for the CRM and Google profile?

No. One login opens everything in your plan. Calls and jobs open in one click, already signed in, and you pay one bill.

How do I know the AI is actually helping?

When a customer books, accepts a quote or pays within 14 days of an AI action, that action gets the credit. Your home screen and a Monday text show the revenue the AI helped bring in, and what didn't work.

Will callers know it's AI?

It speaks naturally in your business name. You choose the name it introduces itself with, and if a caller asks, it tells them it's an AI assistant.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Hindi and Punjabi. It switches when the caller does. Punjabi is newer, so we test it with your real questions during the trial.

What if I go over my calls or texts?

Nothing stops working. Extra answered calls are C$0.75 each and extra texts are C$0.05 each, and you get a text when you pass your plan's limit.

Can customers stop the texts?

Yes. Every text says "Reply STOP to opt out", and anyone who replies STOP is never texted again unless they reply START.
