About

Built for owners who can't always pick up

Loopdesk AI is made by Wayne E Solutions, a digital marketing and software team that works with local businesses in Ontario and Manitoba.

Why we built it

We run Google profiles, ads and websites for local businesses. Again and again, the marketing worked and the phone rang, but nobody picked up. The owner was on a job, with a client or closed for the night, and the caller booked someone else.

Loopdesk AI closes that gap. It answers every call, follows up on the ones that slip through and brings past customers back, so the work you already paid to win doesn't go to waste.
