Terms of service

The basics of using Loopdesk AI.

The service

Loopdesk AI provides an AI phone answering, texting and follow-up service, and on some plans a field service CRM and Google Business Profile management, for the business that signs up.

Free trial

The free trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. At the end of the trial you can choose a plan, change plans or cancel.

Plans and billing

Plans are billed monthly in Canadian dollars, plus applicable tax. Usage over a plan's included calls or texts is charged at the rates on the pricing page. A setup fee applies unless waived on a 6-month term. You can cancel any time.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the information you give the AI to use (services, prices, hours), and for having the right to contact the customers the service texts or calls on your behalf.
