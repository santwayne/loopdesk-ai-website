Pricing questions

Before you pick a plan

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to the front desk, all the time or only when you're busy or closed. Texts go out from a local Canadian number, or from your own number where it can be enabled.

Do I need separate logins for the CRM and Google profile?

No. One login opens everything in your plan. Calls and jobs open in one click, already signed in, and you pay one bill.

What if I go over my calls or texts?

Nothing stops working. Extra answered calls are C$0.75 each and extra texts are C$0.05 each, and you get a text when you pass your plan's limit.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month and you can cancel any time. If you choose a 6-month term, the C$299 setup fee is waived.

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. The 14-day trial needs no card. We set everything up, and you decide at the end whether to keep it.
